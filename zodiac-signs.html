A little guide to the zodiac

Meet the twelve signs.

Explore the symbols, elements, and personality traits traditionally associated with each zodiac sign.

♈

Aries

March 21 – April 19

Bold, energetic, and direct. Aries is often associated with initiative, courage, and a love of getting things started; patience can be a growth edge.

Fire · Cardinal
♉

Taurus

April 20 – May 20

Steady, sensual, and dependable. Taurus themes include comfort, loyalty, and creating lasting foundations; flexibility can help when plans change.

Earth · Fixed
♊

Gemini

May 21 – June 20

Curious, expressive, and quick-minded. Gemini is linked with learning, conversation, and connecting ideas; focus can be a useful balancing practice.

Air · Mutable
♋

Cancer

June 21 – July 22

Caring, perceptive, and protective. Cancer themes include emotional memory, belonging, and nurturing others; clear boundaries help preserve their own energy.

Water · Cardinal
♌

Leo

July 23 – August 22

Warm, creative, and generous. Leo is associated with self-expression, confidence, and bringing joy to a group; remembering to share the spotlight keeps its warmth mutual.

Fire · Fixed
♍

Virgo

August 23 – September 22

Thoughtful, observant, and practical. Virgo themes include care through helpful details, discernment, and craft; self-kindness can soften perfectionism.

Earth · Mutable
♎

Libra

September 23 – October 22

Diplomatic, sociable, and harmony-seeking. Libra is linked with fairness, beauty, and partnership; trusting personal preferences can make decision-making easier.

Air · Cardinal
♏

Scorpio

October 23 – November 21

Intense, perceptive, and resilient. Scorpio themes include emotional depth, privacy, and transformation; openness and trust can help channel that depth constructively.

Water · Fixed
♐

Sagittarius

November 22 – December 21

Optimistic, adventurous, and candid. Sagittarius is associated with exploration, big-picture thinking, and learning through experience; follow-through can ground its many enthusiasms.

Fire · Mutable
♑

Capricorn

December 22 – January 19

Responsible, determined, and strategic. Capricorn themes include patience, ambition, and building something meaningful over time; rest matters alongside achievement.

Earth · Cardinal
♒

Aquarius

January 20 – February 18

Independent, inventive, and community-minded. Aquarius is linked with fresh perspectives and shared ideals; making room for emotional connection can bring ideas closer to people.

Air · Fixed
♓

Pisces

February 19 – March 20

Imaginative, empathetic, and intuitive. Pisces themes include creativity, compassion, and sensitivity; grounding routines and boundaries help support their generous spirit.

Water · Mutable
