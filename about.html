Meet the creator

This is Lili. She loves the stars, the sky, and the moon. She wears a lot of hats: student, administrator, wife, dog and kitty mom, sewist, designer, and the list goes on.

Her deepest passion is seeking within. She believes the answers are already in us if we are silent enough to hear them. Her goal for this website is to give people a fun, introspective place to visit daily, relax, be entertained, and touch base with themselves.
